For the woman I call my forever

My heart, in words

My wife, my forever person, If the northern lights could keep memories, I would ask them to hold the moment you proposed to me—the moment you made a place for me in your future. Out of all the colours that could have filled the sky, I think it would glow emerald green for the hope you gave me, and sapphire blue for the depth of everything I feel for you. We have fought every battle we have faced, and somehow, we have still come out victorious. Not because we never hurt, never feared, or never struggled—but because the hard chapters did not get to erase the beautiful ones. We have crossed difficult ground and kept carrying the possibility of us. That means something to me. You mean something to me beyond what ordinary words can hold. Sometimes I wish I could take every ache from your heart and replace it with the certainty that you are loved. I wish you could see yourself through my eyes: not as someone who must be perfect, but as the woman whose existence has become tangled with my hopes, my prayers, and the future I imagine when the world goes quiet. I don't want our love to be only a beautiful memory. I want it to be a living thing—something we protect with honesty, patience, tenderness, and the courage to understand each other. I want more small moments, more laughter after long days, more dreams that become plans, and more victories we can look back on and say, “We made it through that, too.” When the world is dark, I hope we remember that darkness is not the whole sky. Somewhere above it, the aurora keeps moving: green like new beginnings, blue like a promise that reaches deeper than words. And if life gives us another storm, I hope we meet it not as enemies standing on opposite sides, but as two people who remember why they chose each other. You are my wife in this little universe, my favourite impossible constellation, and my forever person. If I had a thousand lifetimes to search for a home, I would still hope to find the way to you. And if the stars went quiet, if every map disappeared, if the whole universe forgot our names—I would still carry the memory of you choosing me, and I would know which direction my heart was facing. Always yours, Your forever person ✦

If I had to find you in every lifetime, in every universe, and in every version of this world, I would still look for you. And if I found you choosing me again, beneath a sky full of emerald and sapphire light, I would know: I had found my home. ♡
